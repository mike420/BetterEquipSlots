# 🛡️ Better Equip Slots

A character sheet beside the inventory: armour slots, six quick slots, a style for your gear and your stats at a glance. Worn armour no longer takes rows of your backpack, and it all works with a controller.

## ⚠️ Incompatibilities:

  * Not for use with EquipmentAndQuickSlots or CDEV.EquipmentSlots (it replaces both, and stays switched off while either is installed).

<br>

## 🔥 Features:

  * **Equipment slots**: helmet, chest, legs, cape, belt, trinket and ammo. Drag an item in to wear it, or out to take it off. Right-click works both ways.
  * **Six quick slots** for anything, each on any key or key combination (`Z` `V` `B` by default, and the same with `Left Alt`), shown on the HUD next to the forsaken power.
  * **Style tab**: choose how your helmet, chest, legs and cape look, from every piece you have ever held, or hide a piece altogether. Looks only: no armour, no weight, no effects. Other players see it too, even without the mod.
  * **Info tab**: health, stamina, eitr and their regeneration, carry weight, the weapon in hand and its damage, armour, block, resistances, the food you have eaten and the effects on you.
  * **Controller**: D-pad left / right on the hotbar goes on to the quick slots, D-pad up uses one. In the inventory, RB / LB stop on the panel: D-pad to move, A / X / LT / RT as in the inventory, right stick for the tabs.
  * **Padlocks**: a locked inventory cell is never emptied by "stack similar" or by other mods' store and sort buttons.
  * **Ammo**: the next ammo and how many are left above a bow, crossbow or fishing rod on the hotbar, and the next arrows (bolts, bait) put on when the worn ones run out.
  * **Death**: your worn gear lies in the top row of the grave, and *Take All* puts everything back in place and on.
  * **Moving over is painless**: items from EquipmentAndQuickSlots are converted on the first start, and CDEV.EquipmentSlots' slots, styles, locks and settings carry over as they are.

<br>

## 🤝 Works with:

  * Better Archery (quiver in the ammo slot), Equip Multiple Utility Items (extra utility slots), QuickStackStore, Local Craft From Storage Prime, MouseTweaks, Adventure Backpacks, Valheim Plus, BetterUI (quality stars) and Epic Loot (rarity colours).

<br>

## ⚙️ Configuration:

  * Change settings in game (Configuration Manager or the mod settings menu, F7) or in the config file `MagicMike.BetterEquipSlots.cfg`.
  * **General Settings**: **Enable Plugin** (on; local, needs a restart).
  * **Quick Slots**: **Key 1** to **Key 6**, **Alt Modifier** (Left Alt: the HUD shows slots 4-6 while held), **Keys Win Over Vanilla** (off), **Show Key Conflicts** (on), **Gamepad Hotbar** (on).
  * **HUD**: **Show Quick Slots** (on), **Offset**, **Spacing** (8).
  * **Ammo**: **Show On Hotbar** (on), **Auto Equip** (on).
  * **Inventory**: **Cell Locks** (on: the padlocks).
  * **Panel**: **Offset**, **Show Character** (on), **Keep Worn In Inventory** (off), **Show On Key** (off), **Key** (U).
  * **Jewelry**: **Rings**, **Earrings**, **Amulets**: item prefab names worn in those slots (set by the server).

<br>

## 🌐 Multiplayer:

  * A client mod. A server that has it asks every player for it; servers without it let everyone in.
  * **Before uninstalling**, take everything out of the slots: without the mod the game drops it at your feet.

  * Plugin is created to be installed with r2modman or Gale.
