    <td>1.0.8</td>
    <td>
    <ul>
      <li>Added a food bag: a Food section on the Equipment tab with 3 slots (0 to 6, Food Bag → Slots) that take only food, not potions or meads. Auto Eat keeps you fed from it: when one of your three food slots is free, a food of the bag that you are not eating is eaten, and a food about to run out (below 5% of its time) is eaten again if the bag has it, else another food from the bag. Left Alt + F eats from the bag right now. It uses the game's own eating, so a food can still be eaten again only after half of its time. When a food in the bag runs out, the next stack of it from your inventory takes its place (Quick Slots → Auto Refill). New Food Bag settings: Slots, Auto Eat, Refresh Below, Use Another Food, Eat Key and Messages. Translated into all of the mod's languages.</li>
      <li>Added compatibility with Extra Food Slot: with it, the food bag keeps all four food slots fed.</li>
    </ul>
	</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>1.0.7</td>
    <td>
    <ul>
      <li>Added loadouts: four saved sets of worn gear, quick slot items and what is in your hands (weapon, shield, tool) per character, so one press swaps a whole set, weapons included. Four buttons under the character on the Equipment tab: left click puts a loadout on, right click saves what you wear now to it. Each also has a key (Keypad 1-4; hold Left Shift with it to save). With a controller, move onto a button with the D-pad: A puts the loadout on, X saves to it. Items are found in your inventory by kind, preferring the same quality and the best repair; a worn slot the loadout leaves empty is emptied, and any item that is missing is reported. New Loadouts settings: Enable Loadouts, Save Modifier, Key 1 to Key 4 and Include Weapons.</li>
      <li>Saved styles: four buttons on the Style tab save how your helmet, chest, legs and cape look (hidden pieces too) and put it back whatever gear you wear: left click uses a style, right click saves the current one to it (with a controller, A and X). Loadouts can remember the style as well (Loadouts → Include Style, off by default).</li>
      <li>Quick slots: a potion that can't be used yet, because its effect (or another of its kind) is still running, is dimmed on the HUD with the time left shown over it (HUD → Show Cooldown). When the last of a stack in a quick slot is used up, the next stack of the same item from your inventory moves into the slot (Quick Slots → Auto Refill).</li>
      <li>Durability: worn gear at or below 20% durability gets a red tint on the equipment panel, and a message warns once when it drops there. At a workbench the repair button now repairs every item that can be repaired, not one per press. New settings Durability → Warn Below and Repair All At Once.</li>
      <li>Info tab: the armour of each worn piece is listed under Defence, and a Set bonuses section shows how many pieces of each armour set you wear.</li>
      <li>Slots can be left out of the panel while they are empty: Panel → Hidden Slots, or pick a Panel → Preset (Full, Compact, Minimal) to set it and Show Character in one go. A slot with an item is always shown.</li>
      <li>Added compatibility with Jewelcrafting: its rings go in the first Ring slot and its necklaces in the Amulet slot (Jewelcrafting still puts them on, in its own finger and neck slots, and they take no utility slot). With Jewelcrafting alone the panel shows just one ring and one amulet slot. Its socket backgrounds no longer throw on the slot items, which stopped the inventory window updating.</li>
      <li>The loadout buttons and messages, the durability warning and the Info tab's set bonuses are translated into all of the mod's languages.</li>
    </ul>
	</td>
