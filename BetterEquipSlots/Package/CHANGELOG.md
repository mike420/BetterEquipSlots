    <td>1.0.7</td>
    <td>
    <ul>
      <li>Added loadouts: four saved sets of worn gear and quick slot items per character. Four buttons under the character on the Equipment tab: left click puts a loadout on, right click saves what you wear now to it. Each also has a key (Keypad 1-4; hold Left Shift with it to save). Items are found in your inventory by kind, preferring the same quality and the best repair; a slot the loadout leaves empty is emptied, and any item that is missing is reported.</li>
      <li>A potion in a quick slot that can't be used yet, because its effect (or another of its kind) is still running, is now dimmed on the HUD with the time left shown over it. New setting HUD → Show Cooldown.</li>
      <li>Quick slots refill: when the last of a stack in a quick slot is used up, the next stack of the same item from your inventory moves into the slot. New setting Quick Slots → Auto Refill.</li>
      <li>Loadouts also remember what is in your hands (weapon, shield, tool), so they swap weapon sets too. Loadouts saved before this leave your hands alone until you save them again. New setting Loadouts → Include Weapons.</li>
      <li>New Loadouts settings: Enable Loadouts, Save Modifier and Key 1 to Key 4.</li>
      <li>Durability: worn gear at or below 20% durability gets a red tint on the equipment panel, and a message warns once when it drops there. At a workbench the repair button now repairs every item that can be repaired, not one per press. New settings Durability → Warn Below and Repair All At Once.</li>
      <li>Info tab: the armour of each worn piece is listed under Defence, and a Set bonuses section shows how many pieces of each armour set you wear.</li>
    </ul>
	</td>
